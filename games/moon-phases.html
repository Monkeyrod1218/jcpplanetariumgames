JANUARY(enero)
From one new moon to the next is about 29.5 days. The full moon is about halfway (about 15 days). The quarter moons are halfway between a new moon and a full moon. (De una luna nueva a la siguiente hay unos 29.5 días. La luna llena está a la mitad (unos 15 días). Los cuartos están a la mitad entre una luna nueva y una luna llena.)

For teachers and families

The big idea: the Moon shows a repeating pattern of phases about every 29.5 days (one lunar month). Starting from a new moon: first quarter is about 7–8 days later, the full moon about 15 days later (halfway), the third quarter about 22 days later, and the next new moon about 29–30 days later. If a new moon is on January 1, the next is near January 30, and the one after is near February 28 — which is how you predict a new moon in a later month.

Phase pictures (Northern Hemisphere): first quarter is lit on the right half; the full moon is fully lit; third quarter is lit on the left half; the new moon looks dark.
