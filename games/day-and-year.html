One Day, One Year(Un día, un año)

Earth has two motions. See how a spin and a trip around the Sun are different.(La Tierra tiene dos movimientos. Mira en qué se diferencian un giro y un viaje alrededor del Sol.)

One spin = one day (24 hours). One trip around the Sun = one year (about 365 days). Because Earth spins toward the east, the Sun looks like it rises in the east and sets in the west. (Un giro = un día (24 horas). Un viaje alrededor del Sol = un año (unos 365 días). Como la Tierra gira hacia el este, el Sol parece salir por el este y ponerse por el oeste.)

For teachers and families

The big idea: Earth has two separate motions. It rotates on its axis once about every 24 hours — that is one day, and it is why the Sun appears to rise in the east and set in the west. It also orbits the Sun once about every 365 days — that is one year. These are very different speeds: a spin is fast (a day), an orbit is slow (a year).

Distractors targeted: mixing up the daily spin (24 hours) with the yearly orbit (365 days); thinking the Sun circles Earth; and thinking Earth orbits the Sun in a single day.
