Now answer(Ahora responde)

☆☆

For teachers and families

The big idea: Earth rotates once on its axis about every 24 hours. Twelve hours is half of that, so in 12 hours Earth makes one-half turn — 4:00 P.M. becomes 4:00 A.M., and a place moves from the day side to the night side. The Sun never stops shining; half of Earth is always lit, so "night" just means your side has turned away from the Sun.

Distractors targeted: the Sun appears to move but actually stays put while Earth turns; the Sun does not switch off at night; and Earth's trip around the Sun (about 365 days) is a different, much slower motion than its daily spin.
