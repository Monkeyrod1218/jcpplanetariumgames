Summer & Winter Days(Días de verano e invierno)

Pick a season and watch the Sun's path across the sky change.(Elige una estación y observa cómo cambia el camino del Sol por el cielo.)

Sun goes down(El Sol se pone)
Hours of daylight(Horas de luz)
In summer your part of Earth is tilted toward the Sun. The Sun takes a long, high path across the sky, so it is up more hours — longer days. In winter your part is tilted away, so the Sun's path is short and low — shorter days. (En verano tu parte de la Tierra está inclinada hacia el Sol. El Sol toma un camino largo y alto por el cielo, así que está más horas: días más largos. En invierno tu parte está inclinada lejos, así que el camino del Sol es corto y bajo: días más cortos.)

For teachers and families

The big idea: Earth's axis is tilted about 23.5°. In summer your hemisphere leans toward the Sun, so the Sun rises earlier, climbs higher, and sets later — a long, high path — giving more hours of daylight. In winter your hemisphere leans away, so the Sun rises later, stays low, and sets earlier — a short, low path — giving fewer hours of daylight. The tilt (not the distance to the Sun) is what changes day length and the seasons.

One simplification: the exact sunrise/sunset times and path shape depend on your latitude; the game uses friendly, typical values (about 14 hours of daylight in summer and about 10 in winter).
