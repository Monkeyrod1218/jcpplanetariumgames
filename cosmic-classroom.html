Pogue Patrol

A Planets · Moon · Seasons Adventure

Blast off with Rodrigo, Jasmine, and their space pups Sam and Gus! When a routine outing turns into a runaway launch, the crew rockets through our solar system to find their way home. Along the way they learn the order of the eight planets, discover why the Moon looks different every night, and find out how Earth’s tilt gives us the seasons. A full-dome adventure from the John Carl Pogue Planetarium, made for young explorers.

What you’ll explore

  • ✦The order of the eight planets — with a fun way to remember them: “My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Nachos.”
  • ✦Why the Moon looks different each night — the phases of the Moon, from new moon to full moon and back in about 29.5 days.
  • ✦How Earth’s tilt and orbit give us the four seasons — and why summer days are long and winter days are short.
  • ✦Why the Sun seems to rise and set each day, and how the Sun, Earth, and Moon set the rhythm of day and night.

Then play the games

After the show, try these hands-on Grade 3 games to see the science for yourself.

  • Day & Night

    Spin the Earth and watch Austin go from day to night.

  • One Day, One Year

    See how Earth’s daily spin is different from its trip around the Sun.

  • Sun & Shadows

    Move the Sun and watch a shadow change — see what Earth’s spin does.

  • Long Days, Short Days

    Measure daylight and tilt the Earth to find a city’s season.

  • Summer & Winter Days

    See why summer days are long and winter days are short.

  • Moon Phases

    Drag each moon phase to the day it happens on the calendar.
